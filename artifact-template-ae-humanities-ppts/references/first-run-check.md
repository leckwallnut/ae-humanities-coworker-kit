# What a good first-run result looks like

The first-run test in [STARTER-PROMPTS.md](../STARTER-PROMPTS.md) asks for a three-slide lesson on observation and interpretation. Use this page to judge the result. Another good result may choose different layouts and images; what matters is that it passes the checks below.

## Checks

- **File:** an editable 16:9 PPTX (13.33 x 7.5 inches) with exactly three slides. All teaching text is in editable text boxes, not baked into images.
- **Fonts:** Georgia Bold headings, about 38 pt. Arial body text, about 26 pt, with the example quotation around 30 pt. Nothing students must read is smaller than 22 pt. No cursive or decorative fonts.
- **Layouts:** three visibly different compositions, for example two columns on paper, text beside a scene, and an image beside a task. Changing only the background does not count.
- **Content:** slide 1 explains both terms with an example of each. Slide 2 shows the exact sentence, labelled "Classroom example", with two discussion questions and no answers on the slide. Slide 3 gives a short independent task with a clear time.
- **Notes:** slide 1 names the preset, total time, learning outcome and assumptions. Each slide gives its timing and the teacher's and students' actions. Slide 2 holds the suggested answers. Every bundled image is credited by filename.
- **Fit and contrast:** no text overflows its box, crosses a busy part of an image or sits on a low-contrast area.
- **Honest handoff:** the assistant says how the deck was checked and what it could not check.

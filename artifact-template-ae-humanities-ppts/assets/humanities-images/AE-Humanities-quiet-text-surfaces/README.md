# AE Humanities: 12 quiet text surfaces

Twelve blank, opaque backgrounds for widescreen 16:9 Humanities lessons, supplied as high-quality JPEG copies. Built-in image_gen created the paper artwork on 26 October 2026. The original PNGs are kept unchanged in the coworker kit's `Extras/Original images` folder. Text and dashed guides appear only in the visual guide PDF, not in the image assets.

## Use in PowerPoint

1. Set the slide to widescreen 16:9. Insert the selected image as the slide background, or place it behind editable text boxes.
2. Preserve the image's proportions. The 1672 x 941 sources are within one pixel of exact 16:9; proportional fill may crop a tiny amount at the top and bottom. Avoid stretching or cropping away the intended margins.
3. Use Georgia Bold for headings and Arial for passages, notes and quotations. Start with a 38 pt heading and 26 pt passage. The guide uses 30 pt quotations and 24 pt margin notes. Keep student reading text at least 24 pt.
4. Use deep umber `#211B16` on the pale paper. Only the title band in asset 77 uses warm ivory `#F2E8D5` text. Keep body text left aligned, with line spacing around 1.2-1.3.
5. Leave room between the text and the edge wear, border or divider. Use the guide's dashed regions as starting positions; these outlines are not part of the image. Add source attribution in an editable text box or speaker notes as appropriate.
6. Check the actual passage after font substitution, export or resizing. Split or excerpt an overfull passage before shrinking the font. Keep required material in the lesson, notes or a student handout as appropriate.

Word budgets are deliberately comfortable starting ranges, not guaranteed capacities. Word length, paragraph breaks, additional labels, quotations and citations change what fits. They exclude the slide heading. The reference slide size is 13.333 x 7.5 inches. For teaching text that needs longer sustained reading, use a handout alongside a focused excerpt on screen.

## Visual guide and placement data

The visual guide is not installed with the skill; it is in the coworker kit at `Extras/Visual guides/Quiet text surfaces - Visual guide.pdf`. It contains two visual index pages and twelve sample pages. Each sample has a 16:9 slide area at the reference slide size, plus a separate guide footer. Dashed boxes mark the suggested text regions. The prose is an original teaching example, not a quotation from Hemingway or another published writer. Add your own editable text in PowerPoint; the PDF is a placement guide, not a slide template to flatten into classroom slides.

**asset-catalog.json** records the exact file, dimensions, hash, recommended use, word budget and text regions for each asset. Region coordinates are `[x, y, width, height]` as fractions of the slide, measured from the top left. Multiply x/width values by the slide width and y/height values by the slide height. For example, `[0.08, 0.28, 0.84, 0.57]` means start 8% from the left and 28% from the top, using 84% of the width and 57% of the height.

## Validation and provenance

All 12 original PNGs are opaque and approximately 16:9. These checks were run on the originals; the JPEG copies differ from them by about one level in 255 on average. All 30 sample text boxes fit at their declared sizes. The 14-page PDF was rendered and visually inspected. A conservative pixel check found at least 6.68:1 contrast between the specified text color and every pixel in its intended region. This is a source-image screen check; a classroom projector and the completed PowerPoint still need a visual check.

These are AI-generated paper illustrations. Use a short note such as "Background: AI-generated AE Humanities quiet surface, [filename]." Generation prompts are bundled in **README-prompts.md**. No background contains a quotation, handwriting or embedded lesson text.

# Contents and provenance

Version 1.0 was packaged on 27 October 2026 from the AE Humanities PPT skill and its approved teaching resources. Version 1.1, on 28 October 2026, added JPEG copies of the opaque images, the PowerPoint checks, the starter prompts inside the skill and the first-run reference decks.

- **117 images:** 53 original AE Humanities assets; 16 symbolic objects; 12 quiet text surfaces; 12 storytelling images; 24 spacious backgrounds. The 55 images with transparency are the original PNGs, unchanged. The 62 opaque images are high-quality JPEG copies with the original names and dimensions; the original PNGs are in the optional Extras download. Each collection includes its original generation prompts where supplied.
- **20-slide layout library:** editable teaching examples with sample text loads and adaptation notes. Sample passages and fictional profiles are teaching examples.
- **Quiet Words:** the complete 11-slide scary-story writing lesson, including its existing source and teacher notes.
- **Original Unit 1 reference:** not included in the shared kit, because it contains photographs of published textbook pages and other third-party images.
- **First-run reference decks:** the three-slide observation and interpretation lesson, static and animated, made with the skill on 28 October 2026. Its example sentences are original classroom examples.
- **Four-slide motion pilot:** retained unchanged with its existing test record and limitations.

The artwork is AI-generated illustration. Statues, settings and biographical scenes are not documentary evidence. A useful slide-note credit is: "AI-generated AE Humanities artwork, [filename]." Keep any source credits that accompany quoted readings or supplied classroom material. Font binaries and host application/runtime files are not bundled.

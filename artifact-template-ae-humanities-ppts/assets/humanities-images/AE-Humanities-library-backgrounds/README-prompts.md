PowerPoint: use a widescreen slide, right-click the slide > Format Background > Picture or texture fill > Insert, then select the PNG. Add editable text boxes over the dark uncluttered area. Warm ivory (#F2E8D5) works well for text. Keep generous margins from candles, shelves and image edges. The center and bottom-center designs work particularly well for titles, quotes and discussion questions.
